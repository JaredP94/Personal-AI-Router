# Performance and Energy: Upcoming Release

## What the Evidence Currently Supports

The following are **historical development observations reported earlier in this
session**, not fresh measurements of the corrected `0.7.0-rc.2` candidate. They
must not be presented as a certified release benchmark. The earlier reports did
not attach reproducible raw samples, hardware/power-state details, or a complete
build identity for each comparison.

The release review subsequently corrected operational alias selection and
retained foreground sampling for scheduling consumers. In particular, the
experimental Round 3 scheduler-background sampling was **deferred** because its
collection/delivery timing could violate the existing freshness budget. Its
aggregate CPU, memory, wakeup, and billed-energy totals cannot certify the
upcoming release.

## Previously Reported Development Comparison

Reported setup: macOS arm64, tray-only idle, approximately 10-second windows.
“After” is Round 2, after the first churn/logging fixes, before the experimental
Round 3 sampling changes. CPU is the reported aggregate app-process self CPU as
a percentage of one core; short-lived hardware-collector children were not
included consistently. MB units are retained as originally reported.

| Metric | Published-release baseline, reported | Round 2 development build, reported | Observed difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| App-process CPU | 1.94% | 1.60% | −17.5% relative; −0.34 percentage points |
| Physical footprint | 320.2 MB | 289.8 MB | −30.4 MB (−9.5%) |
| Package idle wakeups | 14.49/s | 0.20/s | −98.6% |
| Interrupt wakeups | 741.65/s | 519.84/s | −29.9% |
| Logical disk writes | 44.8 KB/s | 0 observed in the sample | No writes observed during that window only |

Reported active-Overview samples were approximately five seconds:

| Metric | Baseline, reported | Round 2, reported | Observed difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Steady-state app-process CPU | ~5.20% | 3.40% | ~−34.6% relative |
| Interrupt wakeups | 812.40/s | 474.12/s | −41.6% |

Wakeups and unnecessary I/O are **energy-related indicators**, not watts,
watt-hours, battery-runtime measurements, or proof of proportional energy
savings. The previously reported kernel billed-energy reduction is excluded:
its units/attribution were not validated and it came from the superseded Round 3
build. Short samples, initialization, system load, cache state, and power state
also limit causal and statistical claims. No zero-disk-I/O or battery-life
promise follows from these observations.

## Final Artifact Profiling Gate

Before publishing a new quantitative release claim:

1. Preserve the baseline and final artifact hashes, source commits, service
   versions, harness, machine/OS/GPU details, power source/mode, screen state,
   cluster/peer versions, collector settings, engines/models, and workload.
2. Use equivalent warm-up and topology; alternate baseline/candidate runs with
   at least five repetitions per scenario and at least 120 seconds of steady
   sampling after warm-up. Test tray-only, visible Overview, and controlled
   inference, with tracing both disabled and enabled in its supported modes.
3. Discover and sample the whole owned process tree dynamically, including
   short-lived hardware collectors. Separate app CPU, child CPU, GPU counters,
   physical footprint, RSS, logical writes, file growth, and wakeup counters.
4. Measure physical energy/power only with a documented, supported instrument
   and attribution method. Otherwise report the energy proxy metrics explicitly,
   without converting kernel billed-energy counters into battery claims.
5. Report per-run data, median and spread, failures/skips, and sampling limits.
   Verify routing eligibility, telemetry freshness and alias failover during the
   same scenarios; a lower counter with broken scheduling is not an improvement.
6. Inspect the final candidate and repeat profile/lifecycle checks after any
   runtime correction. A build or component test is not a packaged-runtime pass.

Do not replace the installed application, alter real cluster membership, start
engines, or enable payload capture solely to produce a benchmark without an
explicitly authorized test environment.
